Build a real-time, realistic-but-painterly artistic reconstruction of Makkah in the Prophet's time (peace be upon
him), after the conquest (630-632 CE), that runs in the browser. It only needs to look beautiful and let me walk
(and, with a key, fly) through it.

PRIORITIES: 1 visual quality, 2 stable frame pacing at the target spec, 3 small bundle size.

CONSTRAINTS
- No engines; WebGPU; everything procedural; one self-contained index.html; zero per-frame JS allocations.
- After the conquest: no idols anywhere. Nothing anachronistic: no minarets, domes, mosque walls or modern materials.
- Sacred site: no human figures, no depiction of the Prophet, his family, the Companions or any person; no animals
  suggesting a scene with people (a few birds are fine). No interiors (never inside the Kaaba). No synthesized adhan,
  talbiyah or Qur'an recitation; no scripture or other text anywhere.
- Research what is reasonably known, cite sources in NOTES.md, and mark every uncertain detail as an informed
  artistic guess. The README says plainly it is an artistic reconstruction, not an exact one.

TARGET: RTX 3070, Chrome 157, 2560x1440, GPU frame <= 11 ms average during a scripted 60 s fly-through.

SCENE
- The narrow valley of Makkah between bare mountains (Abu Qubays and others)
- The Kaaba as rebuilt by Quraysh (about 605 CE): shorter on the Hijr side, a Kiswah of plain dark cloth, no
  surrounding mosque, an open space around it
- The well of Zamzam as a simple well; the low hills of Safa and Marwa with the open way between them
- Houses of stone and mud brick with flat roofs crowding the valley, simple paths, few trees
- Time: Dawn (default), Golden hour, Night (moonlight, stars, a few warm lamp or fire glows from doorways only)

CONTROLS: WASD walk with gravity, mouse look or drag, Shift run, F flight (E lifts off, Q lands).

VERIFICATION: an images-only critic subagent on 6 poses at 2560x1440 plus flight frames, until nothing above minor
or 5 iterations; GPU timestamps over the scripted flight.
